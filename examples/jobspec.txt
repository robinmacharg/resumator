Lead iOS Engineer - Northwind Health (fictional example)

Northwind Health builds a patient app used by 2 million people to book appointments, see test results and talk to
clinicians. We're hiring a Lead iOS Engineer to lead a team of four.

What you'll do
- Lead the iOS team: planning, code review, pairing and hiring.
- Own the release process and app quality (crash-free rate, accessibility, performance).
- Work with product and design on new patient features, including offline access to records.

What we're looking for
- Strong Swift and SwiftUI; comfortable with UIKit in older screens.
- Experience in a regulated sector (health, finance or similar).
- A track record of improving CI/CD and release automation.
- Care for accessibility (WCAG) and inclusive design.
- Nice to have: some Android or React Native experience.

Remote-first (UK), with a monthly team day in Bristol.
